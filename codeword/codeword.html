How to Play

  1. One person is the giver. Tap Reveal codeword, keep the screen private, and give one single-word clue.
  2. Clues must be one real word. You may use vocal inflection or facial expressions, but no gestures, hand motions, or pointing.
  3. No forms of the codeword. A clue cannot contain any part, root, or variation of it. For example, "win" is not allowed for WINNER.
  4. An illegal clue ends the card. Tap Discard, award no points, and pass the phone clockwise.
  5. Everyone else guesses aloud. Guesses are unlimited.
  6. A chime every 18 seconds allows another clue, up to five clues total.
  7. The first correct guess is worth 5, 4, 3, 2, or 1 point. Keep score yourselves if you want to.
  8. The first person to guess correctly becomes the next giver. If nobody gets it, reveal the answer and pass the phone clockwise.
  9. Clues also cannot spell the codeword, give its first letter, or say what it rhymes with.
